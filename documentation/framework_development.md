# Methodological Rationale for Remote N-of-1 Framework Construction

## 1. Structural Gap Identification
Traditional parallel-group randomized controlled trials (RCTs) isolate aggregate population averages but provide poor direct visibility into single-patient heterogeneous therapeutic responses. This limitation is particularly stark during Guideline-Directed Medical Therapy (GDMT) up-titration sequences for Heart Failure with Reduced Ejection Fraction (HFrEF) populations, where individual blood pressure constraints, chronic kidney disease markers, and treatment compliance variables clash with generalized protocols.

## 2. Framework Core Logic Mapping
This architecture bridges modern digital health monitoring arrays with formal single-case randomized crossover methodologies:
- **Unit of Inference:** Individual patient level via repeated cross-over exposure phases.
- **Data Capture Modality:** Continuous wearable metrics layered over automated home telemetry logs.
- **Clinical Integration Loop:** Incorporates strict pre-specified escalation safety scripts protecting fragile heart failure phenotypes from prolonged tracking errors.
