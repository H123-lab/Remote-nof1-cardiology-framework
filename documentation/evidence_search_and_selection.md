# Comprehensive Methodology Selection and Synthesis Strategy

## 1. Evaluation Scope Mappings
Evidence synthesis was executed across three core scientific research tracks to establish the operational boundaries of this remote framework:
1. **N-of-1 Reporting Architecture Foundations:** Mapped explicitly against established Single-Case Reporting Standards and the CONSORT extension for N-of-1 trials (CENT Guidelines).
2. **Decentralized Tele-Cardiology Blueprints:** Systematic review of clinical telemetry protocols, wearable heart rate variance (HRV) verification constants, and home monitoring platforms.
3. **Precision Cardiovascular Care Metrics:** Electronic Health Record (EHR) medication titration algorithms and guidelines.

## 2. Exclusion Matrix Thresholds
Theoretical paradigms targeting irreversible outcomes (e.g., mortality endpoints, major stroke anomalies, progressive long-latency structural remodeling) or clinically unstable care settings (acute decompensated heart failure) were systematically excluded from this framework, as short-term cross-over washout schedules are unsafe or mathematically invalid for these indications.
