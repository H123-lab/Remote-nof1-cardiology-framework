# Pre-Clinical Master Protocol Template for HFrEF Care Titration

## 1. Randomization Layout
Participating adult individuals are randomly assigned to a balanced cross-over tracking schedule using unique sequence chains generated via verified random seed matrices:
- **Sequence Path Alpha:** Strategy A (4 weeks) -> Stabilization Week -> Strategy B (4 weeks) -> Strategy A -> Strategy B.
- **Sequence Path Beta:** Strategy B (4 weeks) -> Stabilization Week -> Strategy A (4 weeks) -> Strategy B -> Strategy A.

## 2. Telemetry Frequency Mappings
- **Every Morning:** Patient performs blood pressure logging (5 minutes post-rest) and body weight capture via integrated smart scales prior to morning food intake.
- **Continuous:** Wearable tracking devices capture continuous resting heart rate and total daily step configurations.
- **Weekly:** Localized smartphone questionnaire screens log patient-reported symptom scales (dyspnea and tracking fatigue indexes).
